POLICIES

Clear Policies. Better Experience.

Everything you need to know about ordering, customisation, shipping and support.

Shipping Policy

DS ATELIER ships custom apparel after your order is configured and printed. Delivery times vary by product, print type, quantity and destination. We do not guarantee a fixed delivery window for every order.

Processing and production

Custom orders enter production after payment and artwork review. Production time depends on print type, quantity and current studio capacity. Tracking is shared once the order is handed to the courier.

Delivery expectations

  • Dispatch happens after print and pack, not at checkout.
  • Courier transit varies by pincode and serviceability.
  • Some locations may not be serviceable. We will contact you if an address cannot be delivered.

Address responsibility

Please enter a complete, reachable address and a working phone number. Incorrect or incomplete addresses can delay or prevent delivery. Additional courier charges for reattempts or address changes, if any, may apply.

Delays and undelivered orders

Delays can occur due to weather, courier disruption, public holidays or events outside reasonable control. If a shipment is returned undelivered (RTO), we will contact you. Re-shipping, if offered, may require a fresh shipping charge. Refunds for undelivered custom goods are assessed case by case after the return reaches us.

Shipping charges, if applicable, are shown at checkout. Promotional shipping offers, when available, are stated on the relevant campaign or cart screen and are not a standing guarantee in this policy.

Returns & Refunds

Most DS ATELIER products are custom printed to your configuration. Change-of-mind returns, wrong size selections, or preference changes are generally not eligible once production has started.

Quality issues we will review

  • Damaged in transit
  • Defective garment or print
  • Misprint relative to the approved configuration
  • Incorrect item fulfilled

How to raise a claim

Contact support with your order details as soon as you notice an issue. We typically need:

  • Clear photos of the product and the print
  • Packaging photos where relevant
  • Unboxing video where applicable
  • Order ID and a short description of the issue

Please raise quality claims within 7 days of delivery so we can investigate while evidence is available. Approved resolutions may include reprint, replacement or refund, depending on the issue and stock of the selected blank.

Refunds are not automatic. We do not promise replacements or refunds beyond what we can reasonably support after review.

Cancellation Policy

Custom orders can enter production quickly after confirmation. Cancellation is only possible before the order reaches the production or processing stage.

Once printing, pressing or packing has begun, the order cannot be cancelled. If you need to stop an order, contact support immediately. We will confirm whether production has started. There is no guaranteed cancellation cutoff time; eligibility depends on the order’s actual status.

Custom Product Policy

Customised products are made according to the specifications you select: print type, colour, size, quantity, print position and uploaded artwork.

  • You are responsible for choosing the correct size, colour and options.
  • Production begins from the submitted configuration.
  • Custom products are generally not returnable for change-of-mind reasons.
  • Screen colours can differ from physical print. Minor variation is expected.

Sublimation products

Certain sublimation products require light-coloured polyester-based fabric. Product-specific compatibility will be shown on the relevant product page. Sublimation is not assumed to work on every garment in the catalog.

Artwork Policy

You must have the right to use any artwork you upload. DS ATELIER may reject or cancel orders that use artwork we reasonably believe you are not authorised to print.

We do not accept

  • Copyrighted artwork without permission
  • Trademarks or logos without authorisation
  • Pirated or copied designs
  • Infringing or unlawful content

Orders cancelled for unauthorised artwork may not be refundable, because production resources and review time may already have been used.

File quality

  • Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, PDF
  • You are responsible for resolution and clarity
  • Transparent backgrounds are recommended for DTF graphics where applicable
  • Print area and placement follow the product’s available positions

We may contact you if a file cannot be printed as submitted. Colour on screen will not match fabric exactly.

Bulk Order Policy

Bulk and wholesale orders may use different pricing, production timelines and artwork approval steps from single-item orders.

  • A quotation or written confirmation may be required before production.
  • Artwork approval may be required for teams, events and brand runs.
  • Timelines vary with quantity and print type.
  • Customised bulk orders may follow stricter cancellation and return rules than catalog singles.

Minimum order quantities, if any, are confirmed at quotation. This page does not set a universal MOQ.
