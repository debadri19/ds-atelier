DTF Printing

Direct to film, built for detail.

High-opacity whites, fine linework and wash-stable adhesion on cotton, blends and many synthetics.

  • ✓Vivid colour on dark and light fabrics
  • ✓No weeding compared with vinyl
  • ✓Short runs without screen setup
  • ✓Retail finish with a soft hand-feel

Sublimation

All-over colour that becomes the fabric.

Dye-infused prints for sportswear, performance tees and merch that needs full coverage without a heavy ink layer.

  • ✓Breathable prints with no cracking
  • ✓Unlimited colour and gradients
  • ✓Jerseys and gifts ready for teams
  • ✓Fade-resistant under wash and sun
